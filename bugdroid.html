Dead Zebra Bugdroid Plushies

Currently Known Information

The Dead Zebra plushies were released from 2011-2012, with 17 different variants and 4 different colors across various sizes. The plushies came in green, blue, red, and yellow, and different sizes, like keychain (3 inch,) 5 inch, 9 inch, and 20 inches. They were distributed across multiple retailers, like Commonwealth Toy, Novelty Co. Inc, and Good Stuff, and could be found in stores and sometimes arcade machines. In 2011, 13 of the 17 variants were sold, sold in all colors keychain, 5 inch, 9 inch, and only in green as the 20 inch. All these plushies, except the keychain and 20 inches sizes, had turnable heads. In 2012, only the 9 inch size was sold in colors, with no turnable head. You can visually tell the 2011-2012 plushies apart, as there is a bigger white gap between the torso and head to indicate that it's head cannot turn. Although, the presale version of the 20-inch size appears to have a turnable head, with a bigger white gap.
